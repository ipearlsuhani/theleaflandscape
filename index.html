FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a vertical garden? +
A vertical garden is a system where plants are grown or arranged vertically on walls, frames or specially designed structures. It is a practical solution for bringing greenery into limited spaces.
Can you design a garden for a small balcony? +
Yes. Balcony gardens can be customized according to available space, sunlight, plant requirements and your preferred design style.
Do you provide terrace garden solutions? +
Yes. Terrace garden planning can include plant selection, planters, lawn areas, seating zones, pergolas, decking and other landscape elements depending on the site.
What is a preserved moss wall? +
Preserved moss is treated and preserved vegetation designed to maintain its appearance without the regular watering requirements of a living wall. It can be suitable for certain interior spaces with limited natural light.
Do you provide maintenance support? +
Maintenance requirements depend on the selected solution. Living gardens may require regular care, while artificial and preserved solutions generally require less maintenance.
How can I start my project? +
Contact us with your location, space type and requirements. Our team can understand your needs and guide you towards a suitable green solution.
